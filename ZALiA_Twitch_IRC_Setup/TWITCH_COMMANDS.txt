ZALiA - Twitch IRC chat commands
=================================
Type these in the streamer's Twitch chat once TWITCH IRC is connected.
Syntax:  !verb [arg]      Unknown commands are ignored.
Timed effects last ~5 seconds. A shared cooldown throttles all of chat.

PLAYER
  !heal <amount>     heal HP
  !hurt <amount>     chip damage (non-lethal)
  !mp <amount>       give magic
  !drain <amount>    drain magic
  !1up               +1 life
  !refill            full HP + MP
  !curse             slow non-lethal HP drip (stops at 1 HP) [timed]
  !arise             turn Link into a CHICKEN (cucco) [timed]   (aliases: !chicken !cucco)
  !spell <name>      CAST a spell (shows its visual/effect)
                     names: PROTECT JUMP HEAL FAIRY FIRE REFLECT ENIGMA THUNDER SUMMON

BUFFS / SCREEN (all timed)
  !invuln            brief invincibility
  !speed <secs>      speed buff (default 10s, max 60s)   e.g. !speed 20
  !slow              marsh-slow legs
  !shrink / !grow    shrink / embiggen Link
  !shake             screen shake
  !dark              darken room (need fire/candle to see)
  !flip              mirror screen left/right
  !confuse           swap LEFT/RIGHT controls ("drunk")
  !disco             color-cycling overlay
  !party             disco + flip together

ENEMIES / DUNGEON MASTER
  !spawn <enemy> [n] spawn n of a named enemy (default 1, max 6)
                     names: daira moblin goriya zora stalfos bat(ache) atta myu
                     e.g. !spawn moblin 3   or   !spawn 3   (3 of the default)
  !swarm <1-6>       spawn a pack of RANDOM enemies (default 3, max 6)
  !flame  (!heckler) spawn a jumping-flame heckler named after the chatter
  !freeze (!stun)    freeze on-screen non-boss mobs [timed]
  !smite  (!clearscreen)  kill all on-screen non-boss mobs

AUDIO
  !music <track>     play a jukebox track (matches part of the track name)

Notes:
  - Bosses are never affected by !freeze / !smite.
  - Nothing touches the save file. Every effect is runtime-only and reversible.

Examples:  !heal 4   !spawn moblin   !swarm 5   !spell fire   !music forest   !party
