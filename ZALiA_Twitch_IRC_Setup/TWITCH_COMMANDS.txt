ZALiA - Twitch IRC chat commands
=================================
Type these in the streamer's Twitch chat once TWITCH IRC is connected.
Syntax:  !verb [arg]      Aliases in (brackets). Typos get a "did you mean" hint.
Timed effects last the streamer's EFFECT LENGTH setting (5-60 s, default 5) unless
noted. Most timed verbs take a number of SECONDS:  !slow 20   (capped at 60).
With no number, a few pick their own length: !speed 10 s, !root 7-12 s (random),
!confuse and !disorient 10-30 s (random), !stasis 3 s, !challenge the whole palace.
A shared cooldown throttles all of chat, and each chatter also has their own
(30 s by default, user_cooldown= in twitch_config.txt). When the POINTS ECONOMY is
on, most verbs have a price and !points shows your balance; the broadcaster is free.
(Free for everyone: !help !points !top !kills !song !version !suggest !link !vote,
the joke plates and the Rocket League plates.)
The EFFECT TIMERS row on the TWITCH OPTIONS page shows what is running, top-right.
!help in chat puts a one-screen cheat sheet on stream; !help hurt (or chaos,
mobs, fun, rl, mods) shows one page with what each verb does. Points are ON
by default; the POINTS ECONOMY row turns them off.

HELP THE RUNNER
  !heal [n]          full HP, or n quarter-boxes        !mp [n]     full MP, or n points
  !refill (!fairy)   full HP + MP                        !1up        +1 life
  !invuln [s]        invincible for the effect length    !link (!unchicken)  end a chat chicken early
  !protect  !reflect !jump  !life  !fire  !thunder  !enigma  !summon
                     cast that spell for real (its visual plays)   -- also  !spell <name>

HURT THE RUNNER
  !hurt [n]          chip damage: n quarter-boxes (1-8), never lethal (stops at 1 HP)
  !drain [n]         drain magic
  !poison            slow lethal HP drip (can be healed through)  [timed]
  !curse             slow NON-lethal HP drip (stops at 1 HP)      [timed]
  !attrition (!bleed !hunger)  life drips away, never kills         [timed]
  !tax [pct]         (!xp !taxes) take 10% of your XP, or pct% (1-50)
  !dmgup (!doubledamage)  every hit on Link does double damage      [timed]
  !steal (!rob !thief !pickpocket)  borrow one of your items         [timed]
  !catslap [n]       three slaps that knock Link around, no damage  (Tangia "Cat Slap")
                     n = frames to wait before the first slap (60 = 1 s, max 300); always 3 slaps

CHAOS / SCREEN (all timed)
  !speed [s]  !slow        fast / marsh-slow legs
  !shrink (!tiny !small)   !grow (!huge)
  !flip                    mirror the screen           !confuse   swap LEFT/RIGHT
  !disorient               confuse + swap UP/DOWN      !party     disco + flip
  !disco  !shake  !dark    color cycle / screen shake / darken the room. !dark only works in
                           rooms that have a dark version; anywhere else it waits for the next one
  !ice (!icefloor)         the floor is ice            !moon      quarter gravity
  !root                    can't walk or jump (can still swing)
  !stasis [s] (!hold !freezelink)  freeze Link in place, 3 s default, max 10
  !deny <what> [s]         lock a move: spell / jump / stab / crouchstab / downstab /
                           upstab / all.    !undeny [what]  (or  !deny <what> off)  lifts it
  !ghost (!invis)          enemies on screen turn nearly invisible
  !flashbang (!flash)      one white flash; every non-boss enemy on screen takes the smite hit

MOBS / DUNGEON MASTER
  !spawn <enemy> [n]  spawn n (max 6) of: daira moblin goriya zora stalfos bat(ache) atta myu
  !swarm [1-6]        a pack of RANDOM enemies (default 3). Typed on the overworld or in a
                      cutscene it is QUEUED (up to 3) and lands on the next battle screen
  !flame (!heckler)   a jumping flame with the chatter's name over it
  !freeze (!stun)     freeze on-screen non-boss mobs [timed]
  !smite (!clearscreen)  kill all on-screen non-boss mobs
  !crush (!drop !block)  a palace block falls from above Link (palaces only)
  !arise (!chicken !cucco)  turn Link into a CHICKEN [timed]  (needs CUCCO mode on that save)
  !reshuffle (!reroll)   re-roll the seed's enemy randomizer mid-run (rando saves)
  !challenge (!nomercy)  flipped screen + 4 hearts max + no helpful verbs until you leave the palace
                         (a death does not end it; a game over does)

FUN / META
  !music <track>      play a jukebox track (part of the name is enough); !music alone shuffles.
                      Timed: the room's own music comes back when the effect length runs out
  !song (!np !nowplaying !track)   what's playing
  !meth               moon + jump + speed          !quantumentangle (!qe)   a joke, tiny side effect
  !fatality  !friendship  !flawless  !finisher     fire the MK2 splash (no gameplay effect)
  !suggest <idea>     log an idea for the dev      !points (!bal)   your balance
  !top (!leaderboard) richest chatters             !kills (!blame)  who has killed the runner most
  !vote <x> <y> [s]   (!poll) chat picks between two effects, 20 s default (10-60).
                      While it runs, type  x  or  y  (or 1 / 2) in chat to vote. Starting a vote
                      and voting are both free, and so is the winner. It fires in the starter's
                      name; a tie fires both.     e.g. !vote dark flip 30
  !help (!cmds)       cheat sheet on stream        !version         build info
  !donothing  !rip  !blip  !coincidence  !getfed   just toasts

MODS AND THE BROADCASTER
  !ignore <name>      (!mute) that chatter is muted for the whole system: no commands,
                      no votes, no points. Survives a restart (ignored.txt).
  !unignore <name>    (!unmute) let them play again
  Mods skip both cooldowns (the MODS SKIP COOLDOWN row, mod_bypass= in twitch_config.txt).

ROCKET LEAGUE QUICK CHAT (plates only, always free; needs the ROCKET LEAGUE row on)
  !niceshot  !whatasave  !calculated  !savage  !closeone  !okay  !faking
  !gg  !ez  !wow  !noproblem  !thanks

Notes:
  - Bosses are never affected by !freeze / !smite / !flashbang / !ghost.
  - Effects end on death and on loading a file. Turning TWITCH IRC or TWITCH
    REWARDS OFF in the options menu ends everything chat started (panic button).
  - Runtime effects never write to the save. !tax IS permanent (XP is saved) and
    a level-up can be lost to it.

Examples:  !heal 4   !spawn moblin 3   !swarm 5   !spell fire   !music forest   !party 20
